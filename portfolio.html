about.me

I am a Senior Staff Software Engineer at Meta, working on the developer interface of Meta's XR platform: the native runtime-to-engine plugins, the OpenXR integration, the Meta XR Simulator, and AI-agent tooling shipped in the Meta XR SDK. I created the Meta XR Simulator, a desktop OpenXR runtime that simulates physical headsets, led the migration of the Meta XR SDKs to OpenXR, and built Meta XR Operator, the first AI-agent interface for operating XR runtimes and apps. I lead the Agentic AI Task Subgroup in the Khronos OpenXR Working Group. Before Meta, I spent nearly two decades in the game industry and contributed to over 10 AAA titles, going back to the original PlayStation, in roles from Rendering Engineer and Lead Engineer to Technical Architect and Tech Lead Manager.
